AIExplorer Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

AIExplorer is an educational vision app. It lets you take or choose a photo and receive a short identification and learning card.

Photos

When you tap Analyze, the selected image is uploaded to the AIExplorer backend over HTTPS so a vision model can describe it. The server keeps the image in memory for that request only. Images are not written to permanent server storage and are not used to create an account profile.

On-device history

Recent scan names, summaries, timestamps, and local image thumbnails can be stored on your device. You can clear this history in the app. It is not uploaded as a history feed.

Third-party analysis

Image analysis is performed by an OpenAI-compatible vision model called from the AIExplorer server. Do not photograph information you do not want sent for analysis.

No accounts or tracking

AIExplorer does not create user accounts, does not use advertising identifiers, and does not sell personal information.

Children

The app is intended for general educational use. Do not submit photos of other people without their permission.
